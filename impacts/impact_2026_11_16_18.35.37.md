Kafka (Strimzi v1 API, KRaft, один узел, топик); Keycloak (CNPG `keycloak-db` → Keycloak CR, HTTP за Gateway);
Redis (StatefulSet с AOF).

**EN.** Platform components: Envoy Gateway resources on *.localtest.me, otel-lgtm, Catalog on CloudNativePG with a
sync-wave-ordered migration hook, single-node KRaft Kafka, Keycloak on CloudNativePG, Redis.
