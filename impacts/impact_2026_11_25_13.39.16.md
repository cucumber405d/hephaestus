---
name: 15-connector-from-host
description: e2e and drills register the Debezium connector from the host instead of a compose run container
---

**RU.** Контейнер `connector-init` из `docker compose run` не достучался до `http://connect:8083` (на машине Dmitry,
хотя Connect был healthy). Регистрация коннектора теперь идёт с хоста: `register-connector.sh` против
`localhost:18083` после готовности Ordering, как остальные вызовы e2e. Сервис `connector-init` и профиль `init`
удалены из compose.

**EN.** The `connector-init` container started by `docker compose run` could not reach `http://connect:8083` on
Dmitry's machine although Connect was healthy. e2e and the drills now register the connector from the host
(`register-connector.sh` against `localhost:18083`, after Ordering is ready); the `connector-init` service and the
`init` profile are gone from compose.
