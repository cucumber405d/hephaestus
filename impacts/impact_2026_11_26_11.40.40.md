**RU.** Спецификация 16: продолжение 13–15 (Ordering → Forge). Наряд и материал в Forge — event-sourced агрегаты на
собственном event store в PostgreSQL (журнал, конкуренция по версии потока, глобальный порядок через `xid8` и
checkpoint `(tx_id, position)`), журнал как outbox для `ForgeEvent`, проекции `workshop-board`, `smith-load`,
`stock-ledger`, replay, upcasting, time travel, учения. Без Debezium; платформа за флагом `cqrsEventSourcing`.

**EN.** Design of 16: continues 13–15 (Ordering → Forge). Work order and material become event-sourced aggregates on
an own PostgreSQL event store (journal, stream-version concurrency, global order via `xid8` with a `(tx_id,
position)` checkpoint), the journal doubles as the outbox for `ForgeEvent`, projections, replay, upcasting, time
travel, drills. No Debezium; platform behind the `cqrsEventSourcing` flag.
