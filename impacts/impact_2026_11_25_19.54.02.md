name: 15-executable-scripts
description: Scripts of 15 executable in git; CI checks the recorded mode
---

**RU.** `verify` и `checks` в CI падали за 7 с: часть скриптов 15 попала в git без бита исполнения (Windows,
`core.fileMode=false`). Бит выставлен через `git update-index --chmod=+x`, job `checks` теперь проверяет режим
файлов `scripts/**/*.sh|py` в индексе git.

**EN.** CI's verify and checks failed after 7 s: some scripts of 15 were committed without the executable bit
(Windows, core.fileMode=false). The bit is set with `git update-index --chmod=+x`, and the checks job now verifies the
mode git records for `scripts/**/*.sh|py`.
